## WHAT I CHECKED AND RULED OUT
  memory_layout.h CYGHEAP_STORAGE_LOW/INITIAL/HIGH are NOT arch-conditional
  (0x800000000 / 0x800300000 / 0xa00000000, no #ifdef). x22 holding
  0x800300000 confirms the child is using the correct constants. So this
  is NOT another instance of the x86-fallthrough pattern.

## STRONGEST REMAINING LEAD, WITH ITS CAVEAT STATED
  x18 = 0x0000000000000000 IN THE FAULTING CHILD THREAD.
  On Windows on Arm x18 IS the TEB and is platform-reserved; this runtime
  depends on it (the __getreent fix earlier this programme changed
  'mrs tpidr_el0' to x18 precisely because x18 is the TEB here). A child
  thread resumed with x18 = 0 would have no TEB.
  CAVEAT, NOT YET ELIMINATED: x18 is platform-reserved, and it is not
  established that GetThreadContext reports it faithfully rather than
  returning zero for a register Windows manages itself. I have ONE sample
  and no control. DO NOT TREAT THIS AS A FINDING. The control needed is
  a captured context from a thread known to be healthy in the same
  process, compared against this one.
