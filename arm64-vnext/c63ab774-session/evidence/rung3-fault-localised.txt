## WHY THIS IS RECURSION, NOT A DEEP CALL CHAIN
  PC is at calloc+0x4 (its prologue probe).
  LR is at calloc+0x3C, i.e. the return address of the 'blr x2' INSIDE calloc.
  So calloc dispatched through a function pointer and control came back
  round into calloc's own prologue. calloc is calling itself indirectly.
  The stack probe is simply the first instruction to notice the stack is
  exhausted -- it is the symptom, not the cause.

  This is the malloc-interposition path: a flag byte selects between the
  internal allocator and an exported/overridden one, and the ARM64 build
  appears to resolve the override back to itself. NOT YET PROVEN -- the flag
  and the function pointer have not been read at runtime.

## CONSISTENT WITH THE STACK-RESERVE EXPERIMENT
  2 MB reserve  -> STACK_OVERFLOW (guard page hit sooner)
  64 MB reserve -> ACCESS_VIOLATION (recursion runs longer, dies differently)
  Unbounded recursion explains both, and explains why more stack does not help.
