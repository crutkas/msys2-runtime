Add pre-build_argv check for quote-free filler+long corpus

The size-sweep proved corruption occurs for ANY nonzero filler padding
regardless of content/length, using a filler ('P'*N) with no glob or
quote characters. Direct reading of globify() (dcrt0.cc:207-210) shows
it early-returns immediately whenever the word contains none of the
glob-special characters and does not start with '~' -- true for both
the plain filler and $long themselves. quoted() is likewise never
invoked (no quote characters in this corpus). Since $long is always
the final argument, build_argv()'s own scan loop performs no in-place
write to it either (the terminating write is skipped when the cursor
is already at the buffer's pre-existing NUL). This means, for this
specific corpus, build_argv()/quoted()/globify() cannot be the
corruption source -- so add a direct pre-build_argv raw-buffer diff
(reusing the NUL-anchor technique from the prior bisection step) at
padding 0/16/256 to determine whether the corruption already exists
before build_argv() runs for this quote-free/glob-free case.
